### Finish levels
- **Eyebrow:** Drywall finish levels
- **H2:** What does "Level 5" actually mean?
- **Answer:** Drywall finishes are graded from Level 0 to Level 5 under an industry standard (GA-214). Most homes get a Level 4 finish. Level 5 adds a skim coat over the entire surface, so walls look uniform under strong side light, glossy paint or big windows.

| Level | What it is | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Board hung, no taping or finishing | Temporary construction |
| 1 | Tape set in compound at joints | Attics, above ceilings, hidden areas |
| 2 | Tape plus one coat over joints and screws | Garages, behind tile |
| 3 | Two coats over joints and screws | Walls getting heavy texture |
| 4 | Three coats, sanded smooth | Most homes with flat or matte paint |
| 5 | Level 4 plus a skim coat over the whole surface | Glossy paint, strong side light, premium finishes |

- **Our standard:** We finish to Level 4 as standard and recommend Level 5 wherever the lighting or paint calls for it. [CONFIRM]

### FAQ (drywall)
1. **How much does drywall repair cost in Ottawa?** (same answer as Home)

2. **How much does drywall installation cost?**
   Drywall installation, taping and finishing are priced by the square footage of board, plus ceiling height, board type and finish level. A basement or addition is usually quoted as one package. We measure on site and give you a firm written price.

3. **How long does drywall mud take to dry?**
   Each coat of joint compound usually needs about 24 hours to dry, depending on humidity and temperature. That's why a proper three-coat finish takes several days, even on a small job. Faster-setting compounds can speed up the early coats when the schedule is tight.

4. **Can you match my existing ceiling texture?**
   In most cases, yes. We blend repairs into flat, stipple and other textured ceilings so the patch isn't noticeable. If a ceiling has been patched many times, a full skim coat or texture removal is often the better long-term fix.

5. **What causes nail pops and cracks?**
   Most nail pops and hairline cracks come from wood framing shrinking and shifting as humidity changes through the seasons, which is common in Ottawa homes. We reset or replace the fastener, then re-tape and re-coat so the repair stays hidden.

6. **Is popcorn ceiling removal messy?** (flag)
   It can be, which is why we contain the room first: plastic on walls and floors, sealed doorways and vents, and dust extraction while sanding. Ceilings in older homes are tested for asbestos before any scraping. [CONFIRM]

7. **Do I need to move my furniture?**
   Clear what you can from the work area. We cover and protect anything that stays, and put the room back the way we found it.
